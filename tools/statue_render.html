Statue tower sightlines

Two statues of 7 towers, corner to the centre of the circle, a bench at the centre. The defaults are the artist's final layout (23 Oct 2026): her heights and her six spacings, placed on the solver's rails so neighbouring silhouettes touch from the bench. "Reset to the solved layout" places them so that, seen from the bench, neighbouring silhouettes exactly touch. Each of the four viewpoints below owns 14 faces; its picture is projected from that eye onto them and cut into the wood, lit by the LEDs behind. Lengths in mm.

Pictures and cuts

White (or outside the DXF shapes) stays wood, the rest is cut and lit. A picture is stretched to the silhouette of its 14 faces as seen from the viewpoint, shrinkable from each side. Moving towers re-projects the cuts.

Export DXF for the laser cutter

One DXF per face, mm, R12: layer OUTLINE is the plate, layer CUT the holes. Files are named statue-tower-face; faces A and B look at the bench (A on the left as seen from it), C and D look outwards (C on the left as seen from behind).
